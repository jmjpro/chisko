Revenue logic should match the lead quality you deliver

  • Basic leads capture quote intent and contact details.
  • Qualified leads add bill data, household details, plan-fit estimate, and contact consent.

The most persuasive market story is: ~2.7M households form the illustrative market base, a realistic 1% to 3% early capture gives 27k to 99k reachable households, and monetization rises with intent and data quality.
