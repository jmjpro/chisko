# Channel Experiment Plan

## Goal
Find the cheapest repeatable channel for the first 1,000 qualified leads, where supplier revenue per lead is greater than acquisition cost.

## Core metric
Profit per qualified lead = supplier payout per qualified lead - fully loaded acquisition cost per qualified lead.

Track these separately:
- Visitor to lead conversion rate.
- Lead to qualified lead rate.
- Qualified lead to paid lead rate.
- Cost per visitor.
- Cost per lead.
- Cost per qualified lead.
- Supplier payout per qualified lead.

## Assumptions to test
- Consumer pain is real.
- Bills or usage uploads improve lead quality.
- Personalized recommendations increase conversion.
- Some channels produce much higher intent than others.

## Channel mix to test

### 1. SEO and content
Use comparison content, bill explainer pages, supplier pages, and “which plan fits me” posts.

Hypothesis: lower cost, slower ramp, strongest long-term economics.

Test:
- 10 to 20 pages targeting high-intent queries.
- One landing page per intent cluster.
- Include bill-upload CTA and “estimate savings” CTA.

Success signal:
- Cost per qualified lead under target after initial indexing.
- Organic leads convert at higher rates than paid traffic.

### 2. WhatsApp and community sharing
Use neighborhood groups, family groups, apartment groups, expat groups, and local community admins.

Hypothesis: best for trust-heavy consumer leads if the offer is framed as savings help, not ads.

Test:
- Post manually in 20 to 30 relevant groups.
- Offer free bill checks or savings reviews.
- Ask happy users to forward a referral link.

Success signal:
- Lead cost near zero to very low.
- High completion rate on bill upload.

### 3. Referral loop
Let users share their recommendation result with a friend or spouse.

Hypothesis: savings results are naturally shareable and can create low-cost growth.

Test:
- After recommendation, prompt: “Send this savings estimate to someone who pays the bill.”
- Offer a small incentive or value add.

Success signal:
- At least 10% of qualified users share.
- Referred users convert better than cold users.

### 4. Partner channels
Work with electricians, solar installers, property managers, relocation agents, accountants, and community leaders.

Hypothesis: small but high-trust lead flow.

Test:
- 10 partner outreach conversations.
- Offer co-branded savings checker.
- Pay a referral fee or share revenue.

Success signal:
- Partner-sourced leads are cheaper than paid social.
- Lead quality is high enough to justify rev share.

### 5. Paid search
Target high-intent searches like switching, bill comparison, electricity plan, night rate, EV charging, and supplier names.

Hypothesis: expensive but high intent.

Test:
- Small daily spend.
- Separate ad groups by intent.
- Send traffic to one focused landing page per intent.

Success signal:
- Positive contribution margin after payout.
- Clear keyword clusters with acceptable CPL.

### 6. Offline activation
Use printed flyers, community noticeboards, local events, apartment lobbies, and direct outreach.

Hypothesis: helpful in Israel if tied to a concrete savings promise.

Test:
- Pilot in 2 to 3 neighborhoods.
- Use QR code to a simple bill check flow.
- Track scan-to-lead conversion.

Success signal:
- Cheap scans and decent completion rates.
- Good trust and referral spillover.

## Experiment design
Run each channel with a strict budget cap and a single success metric.

Suggested setup:
- 2 weeks per test block.
- One primary channel owner per test.
- One landing page per channel.
- One consistent offer.

Budgets:
- SEO/content: mostly founder time + small tool cost.
- Community/referral: minimal cash, mostly time.
- Partner outreach: small travel and admin cost.
- Paid search: fixed test budget.
- Offline: small print budget.

## Decision rules
Keep a channel only if it meets all three:
- Qualified lead cost is below supplier payout.
- Lead quality is stable.
- It can plausibly scale beyond the first 1,000 leads.

Drop or pause a channel if:
- It produces lots of low-quality leads.
- It requires too much manual work per lead.
- Supplier payout cannot cover the all-in cost.

## Recommended priority order
1. Community and referral loops.
2. Partner channels.
3. SEO and content.
4. Paid search.
5. Offline experiments.

This order gives you the best shot at cheap early signal before you spend heavily on paid traffic.

## First 30-day plan
Week 1:
- Build 3 landing pages.
- Set up tracking.
- Prepare bill upload and recommendation CTA.

Week 2:
- Run community and partner outreach.
- Launch one referral flow.
- Publish first content pages.

Week 3:
- Start a small paid search test.
- Try 1 to 2 offline neighborhood experiments.

Week 4:
- Compare cost per qualified lead by channel.
- Cut the worst performers.
- Double down on the best 1 or 2 channels.
