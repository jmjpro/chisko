# A single lead-form submission fans out Referrals to multiple suppliers

Lead capture is two steps, not one. Step 1 (name, phone, optional email) submits immediately on its own action and creates the Lead plus one form-handoff Referral for the supplier whose card the user clicked — no extra click required to capture the core lead. Step 2, shown afterward in the same dialog, lists every other supplier in the Fan-Out Scope individually (logo + name, each pre-checked, individually uncheckable) and requires its own explicit confirm action; dismissing the dialog without confirming step 2 creates no further Referrals despite the pre-checked state. Confirming step 2 creates one form-handoff Referral — each with its own Consent and timestamp — per supplier left checked. Fan-Out Scope is the Recommendation's full eligible-plan pool when a Recommendation exists for the session (including plans never rendered on screen, e.g. still-collapsed alternatives), or every active Supplier when no Recommendation exists for the session (the standalone plans browsing page, as currently scoped). We split fan-out into its own confirm step — rather than bundling it into the initial submit, or treating dismissal as consent — so the core lead is never blocked on reviewing the fan-out list, while Consent for the additional suppliers remains its own explicit action rather than implied by a pre-checked default.
